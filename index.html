About Trust Move Courier & Cargo

Trust Move Courier & Cargo is a Kathmandu-based international courier and cargo service provider dedicated to bridging Nepal with the rest of the world. Operating from our key locations at Mahadevsthan, Koteshwor and Saatghumti, Thamel, we specialize in delivering documents, personal packages, and commercial cargo safely, efficiently, and professionally.

Our commitment focuses on customer satisfaction, secure transit management, and clear, transparent communication throughout every step of the shipping process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our international shipping services.

How do international shipping rates work?

Rates depend on the destination country, chargeable weight (actual weight vs. volumetric weight), and the type of shipment (documents vs. commercial cargo). Contact us or submit a quote request for exact pricing.

How can I track my shipment?

You can easily track your package using our online tracking tool on this website by entering your unique tracking number. For demonstration purposes, you can test it with number TM-DEMO-001.

Do you offer door-to-door delivery?

Yes, we provide safe door-to-door pickup from your location in Kathmandu and delivery straight to the doorstep of your recipient overseas in major destination countries.

Can you ship documents securely?

Absolutely. We handle urgent academic, legal, and business documents with priority handling and secure packaging to ensure they arrive intact and on time.

How do I get an accurate price quote?

You can fill out our online Get a Quote form with your shipment specifications or contact our offices directly via phone or visit our branches in Koteshwor or Thamel.

How can I contact Trust Move Courier & Cargo?

You can reach us at our branches in Mahadevsthan, Koteshwor and Saatghumti, Thamel, or call us directly at 01-4500711, 9744475840, or 9810966584.
